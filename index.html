Privacy Policy — James Allen Daily

Last updated: 14 October 2026

James Allen Daily ("the app") is published by August. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so that it is there the next time you open it:

  • the days you bookmark, the ratings you give, and how often you have opened each day;
  • the passages you highlight and the comments you write;
  • your settings: highlight colours and labels, theme, and the daily notification time.

None of this leaves your device through the app.

Backups made by Android

If you have turned on Android's own backup in your device settings, Android may include this app's data in the backup it keeps in your Google account, and restore it when you set up a new device. That backup is made and controlled by Android and Google, not by the app or its publisher, who never receive it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Exporting your data

The app can write your bookmarks, ratings, highlights and comments to a file when you choose "Export a backup". You pick where that file is saved, and what happens to it afterwards is up to you.

Searching the web

When you select text and tap the search button, the app hands the selected words to your device's search app or browser. That only happens when you tap the button, and the search is then handled by that app under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to show the optional daily meditation, and only if you turn it on.
  • Run at startup: used only to set the daily notification again after the device restarts.

Children

The app is a book reader with no data collection and is suitable for a general audience.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything it has stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
